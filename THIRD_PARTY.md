## AI-generated content

Judy's dialogue is generated by a large language model chosen by the user. Her voice is generated with XTTS v2 and RVC and imitates the original voice actor, who has not endorsed this project. The RVC voice model was trained by the author on voice lines taken from the game's audio, and the XTTS speaker profile was derived from a short clip of the same source. The clip itself is not distributed in this repository; the derived speaker file (`judy_voice_latents.pt`) is hosted in the Hugging Face voice bundle.
